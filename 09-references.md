## Research still required before implementing pickle rules

- Identify exact relevant FSSAI regulations and amendments, with rule-level citations.
- Review current Legal Metrology packaged-commodity requirements and exceptions.
- Establish which label language(s), declaration placement, net quantity, manufacturer details, allergens, dates and other fields apply to the chosen product and pack.
- Confirm effective dates, statuses, exemptions and precise product/channel scope.
- Distinguish registration/licensing guidance from any claim of official determination.
- Obtain human review of the resulting rule mappings before running regulatory checks.

## Source limitations

Vendor self-descriptions or older compendia do not independently establish a current legal requirement. Future-dated rules must be distinguished from rules already in force. The project's regulatory engine and recall guidance remain **unbuilt**.
