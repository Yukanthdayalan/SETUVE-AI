# MVP Scope and Roadmap

## Round 1 — Concept and submission (current)

**Deliver:** Documentation, problem understanding, proposed workflow/architecture, risk boundaries, feasibility and research plan, presentation, supporting references.

**Do not deliver/claim:** Functional application code, a deployed platform, model benchmarks, live official government integration, regulatory certification or pilot impact.

## Round 2 — Build only if selected

### MVP freeze

- 1 product: mango pickle in a retail jar.
- 1 fictional or consenting producer profile.
- 3 interface modes: English, Kannada, mixed; **text mandatory**.
- Voice enabled conditionally after testing.
- 1 limited official rule set reviewed for relevance to the jar.
- 1 versioned draft label PDF template.
- 2 pricing channels: local sale + illustrative marketplace.
- 1-2 simulated batches/orders and incident.
- 1 Buyer Readiness Report.

### Suggested build sequence

1. **Foundation:** language selection, product form, typed ingredient capture/confirmation, local demo data.
2. **Business logic:** date/applicability rule filtering, label draft with unresolved warnings, cost calculator.
3. **Traceability:** recipe and label versions, batch/order linkage, human approval gates.
4. **Agent workflow:** orchestrate prompts and safe tools; recover from missing information.
5. **Output:** readiness report, export, audit view and incident draft/escalation links.
6. **Validation:** real Kannada/mixed recordings, rule-review scenarios, regression tests and demo rehearsal.

**Do not sacrifice safety and correctness testing to add extra features.**

## Beyond Round 2

- Food category expansion only after legally scoped rules are reviewed.
- Authorized verification integrations only with actual permitted access.
- Partner-specific buyer requirements and live channel fee models.
- Usability pilot with consenting micro businesses.
- Additional languages, multi-producer dashboards and offline-first operations.

## Open decisions

- Team members and exact competition submission requirements.
- Public vs private repository visibility and project license.
- Confirmed registration and label rulebook owners/reviewers.
- Speech vendor, costs, consent and dataset requirements.
- Product-label language and regional format specifications.
- Whether the working name Setuve AI has naming/trademark conflicts.
- Pilot data access and genuine producer interviews.
