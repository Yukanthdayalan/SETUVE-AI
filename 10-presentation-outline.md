## Claims policy for slides

Use labels such as **Proposed**, **Planned**, **Under Evaluation**, or **Round 2 Roadmap**. Do **not** show mock-up screenshots as functional software or fabricated speech/model test metrics as measured results. Label fictional numbers in the pricing demo clearly.

If a 10-slide template is mandatory, combine slides 3+4 and 11+12, keeping slide 7 (user journey) and slide 10 (differentiation) legible.
