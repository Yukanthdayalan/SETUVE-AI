# Proposed Solution — Setuve AI

**Setuve AI: From Local Kitchens to Larger Markets**

## The idea

A multilingual, agentic-AI-assisted **market-readiness workspace** for small food producers. The producer can describe a product naturally; Setuve AI guides data collection, organises documents, prepares drafts and identifies next actions.

## What the producer gets

1. A confirmed, structured product record with ingredients and quantities.
2. Provisional, source-linked registration guidance, not a registration decision.
3. A draft packaged-food label with dated and applicable checks.
4. A claims and shelf-life evidence checklist.
5. A channel-contribution estimate with producer-editable costs.
6. Batch and label-version records linked to stock and recorded sales.
7. A consolidated **Buyer Readiness Report**.

## Three language choices

- **English:** English interface and output; English voice after benchmark acceptance.
- **Kannada:** Kannada interface and output; Kannada voice after benchmark acceptance.
- **Mixed Kannada–English:** Natural code-switched input; output preference separate from the spoken language.

**Text input must work regardless of the chosen speech provider**. The producer confirms parsed quantities, units and ingredients before they are saved.

## Why an agentic workflow?

Rather than only producing advice, an orchestrator could ask for missing net weight, save a confirmed field, rerun affected draft label checks, recompute illustrative costs and regenerate readiness gaps. Its ability to act must remain restricted to approved tools and explicit human decision points.

## Human control

An agent can *draft* and *recommend*; the producer or appropriately qualified reviewer signs off labels and prices, and approves outreach/sharing. Official filings, product-safety decisions and laboratory results remain outside autonomous scope.

## Final deliverable

The **Buyer Readiness Report** should separate:

- **LAW** — applicable verified legal requirements
- **BUYER POLICY** — identified requirements of a named channel or buyer
- **BEST PRACTICE** — optional recommended controls

Each item includes evidence, confidence/applicability, source details, reviewer status and next action. “Ready” means readiness *for review and preparation*, not certification or acceptance.
