# End-to-End Demonstration Journey

**Persona:** Fictional Kannada-speaking home-based mango pickle producer.  
**Context:** Preparing a sample 20-jar product for larger buyers.  
**Status:** Proposed scripted demo, **not user-tested**.

## 1. Select a language

Choose **English**, **Kannada**, or **Kannada + English**. A text input always remains available; voice use depends on validation.

## 2. Describe the product

> “ನಾನು 20 pickle jars ಮಾಡಿದ್ದೀನಿ, 2 kg ಮಾವಿನಕಾಯಿ ಮತ್ತು 200 grams chilli powder use ಮಾಡಿದ್ದೀನಿ.”

Proposed extraction: `20 jars`, `mango 2 kg`, `chilli powder 200 g`. The application asks the producer to **confirm**, not assumes the transcript is correct. Other ingredients are still unknown.

## 3. Register the business details

Ask about activity, scale and relevant FSSAI criteria. Show provisional registration guidance, what cannot be inferred and a link to official FoSCoS.

## 4. Draft a label

Create a PDF preview with placeholders for missing mandatory fields. Run only supported applicable rules, making unresolved checks visible and requiring human approval of final label data.

## 5. Check evidence

If the producer says, “Add immunity boosting” or “Set shelf life to 12 months,” record as **proposed/unverified**, flag for appropriate evidence or expert review, and prevent unsupported finalization.

## 6. Compare channels

Show a local-sale and one illustrative online-marketplace contribution calculation. Label all commission, delivery and returns assumptions as **illustrative**, with no claim of live fee retrieval.

## 7. Create and trace a batch

Create example `MP-001` with product and label version references, stock movements and possible recorded sales. Demonstrate an incident lookup without sending an official recall.

## 8. Review readiness

Create the report: **what is documented, what is pending, what needs human approval, and what to do next**.

## 9. Show an actual agentic dependency

Change the recipe ingredient quantity. The system should retain historical MP-001/Label v1, but mark dependent **future** label and cost/readiness work as stale until updated and signed off.

## Proposed demo close

> “Setuve AI helps a micro producer prepare a traceable, reviewable pathway toward larger markets. It does not certify compliance or guarantee marketplace onboarding.”
