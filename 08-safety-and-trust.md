# Safety, Human Oversight and Trust Boundaries

## What Setuve AI can be designed to do

Organize product details, draft documents, run reviewed checks, identify missing information, calculate illustrative commercial scenarios, log versions, prepare incident communications and show official next steps.

## What it must not claim or do

- Certify compliance or food safety; replace a laboratory, regulator, lawyer or qualified food-safety professional.
- Issue an FSSAI registration/licence or independently verify official records without an authorized live integration.
- Invent shelf-life periods, nutrition analyses, medical/health claims, laboratory results or regulatory citations.
- Treat a private product recipe as shareable with buyers by default.
- Finalize a label, publish a price, submit an official filing, send external messages or share buyer documents without the required human action.
- Automatically treat a recipe change as rewriting historic stock/batches; retain audit history.

## Approval and provenance states

`not_provided` → `self_declared` → `document_supplied` → `manual_reviewed` or `officially_verified` (only after a genuine official check).

**`simulated` is never equivalent to `officially_verified`.** A syntactically valid FSSAI number is not proof of active status.

A draft label passes through `draft → needs_review → approved_by_human`. Any applicable recipe change triggers a new revision; prior approvals remain historic records, not current approvals for new products.

## Regulatory knowledge quality

- Record source, publication date, in-force date, affected products, packaging, exceptions and reviewer.
- Distinguish **LAW**, **BUYER POLICY** and **BEST PRACTICE**; do not conflate.
- If legal context is unknown, show “requires review” and safe links, not definitive compliance.
- Regulatory source pages and individual amendments need periodic re-review.

## Incidents and recalls

For complaints, help locate batches and orders, draft an internal checklist and display escalation instructions. An authorized human must take necessary official action; do not delay urgent duties while waiting for a chatbot. Refer to **FoSCoS** and relevant legal/expert processes; in-app drafts are **not official recall filings**.

## Privacy

- Get meaningful consent for voice recording and AI processing; minimize retention.
- Keep private recipes, pricing and uploaded documents access-controlled.
- Distinguish internal workspace data from shareable Buyer Readiness Reports.
- Review third-party AI processing, storage, deletion, encryption and audit access before any real deployment.
- Use fictional or consented demo data; do not publish registration numbers, home addresses or secret formulas in a public repository.
