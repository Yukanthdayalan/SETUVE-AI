# Competition, Alternatives and Positioning

> **Status: preliminary positioning only. Competitor capability matrix and independence checks are pending research.** Do not present this document as proof of a unique invention.

## Categories to compare

| Category | Example | What to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Official portal | [FoSCoS](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/) | Official registration/licensing functions vs producer-preparation assistance |
| Food-label/compliance solutions | FoodOS360 and regional food consultancy tools | Labels, registrations, product data and costs; vendor capabilities require validation |
| Food-safety workflow software | FoodDocs, FoodReady | Food safety plans, evidence, audits; check geographic suitability and pricing |
| Traceability and recall products | TraceX, FoodLogiQ/Trustwell | Batch/order traceability, incident workflows, integrations |
| General AI/cross-industry analogies | Evidence automation and regulatory monitoring tools | Illustrations of workflow techniques, not direct substitutes |

## Proposed gap to test

An accessible, Kannada/English code-switched **micro-producer journey** connecting data confirmation, source-linked applicable registration guidance, an evidence-aware label draft, cost scenarios, traceable versions and a Buyer Readiness Report — with human approval and dependency-aware revalidation.

This combination is a **design hypothesis**, not a demonstrated market gap, patented innovation or verified first-of-its-kind product. Claims of comprehensive competitive differentiation require documented testing against actual vendor products and current offers.

## Pitch language

**Use:** “We propose a connected, local-language workflow designed for micro producers, bringing previously scattered preparation tasks into one auditable journey.”

**Avoid:** “No other platform does this,” “100% compliant,” “AI automatically certifies products,” or “guaranteed to get your pickle into Amazon/retail.”
